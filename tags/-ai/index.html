93 Percent People approved Claude Code’s permission prompts roughly 93% of the time. Anthropic shared that number to make a point: the more prompts you see, the less you read them, and eventually the human check stops being much of a check. Anthropic describes two fixes. One is to let another system make the routine calls: auto mode, where a classifier decides which actions are safe enough to approve. The other is to limit what the agent can do in the first place, so some actions aren’t possible at all.
